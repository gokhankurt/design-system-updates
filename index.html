A re-imagination of the Green Charging Figma design using oblique 3D projection. Each energy series occupies its own depth plane – PV production in back, household consumption in front – rendered with the painter’s algorithm. Hover a curve or legend entry to highlight it; move the cursor across the chart to see per-series values at each hour.
